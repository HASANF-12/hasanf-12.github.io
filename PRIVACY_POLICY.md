# Privacy Policy for CoinFlow

**Last Updated: [2026-02-01]**

## Introduction

CoinFlow ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application CoinFlow (the "App").

## Information We Collect

### Data You Provide
- **Financial Information**: Transactions, wallet balances, budgets, goals, and related financial data
- **Personal Information**: Name (if provided during onboarding)
- **Receipt Photos**: Images you capture or upload for receipts
- **Notes and Tags**: Additional information you add to transactions

### Data Collected Automatically
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version
- **Usage Data**: How you interact with the app
- **Advertising ID**: Used by AdMob for personalized advertising (Android Advertising ID)

## How We Use Your Information

- **App Functionality**: To provide core features like transaction tracking, budget management, and goal setting
- **Data Storage**: All your financial data is stored locally on your device
- **Advertising**: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use your Advertising ID for ad personalization
- **Improvements**: To improve app performance and user experience

## Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage**: All your financial data (transactions, wallets, budgets, goals) is stored locally on your device using local storage
- **No Cloud Sync**: We do not upload, sync, or transmit your financial data to external servers
- **Security**: Your data is protected by device-level security (PIN protection) and local encryption
- **Your Control**: You can delete all data by uninstalling the app

## Third-Party Services

### Google AdMob
- We use Google AdMob to display advertisements
- AdMob may collect:
  - Advertising ID
  - Device information
  - Location data (approximate)
  - App usage data
- For more information, see: [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings

### Exchange Rate API
- We use exchange rate APIs to provide currency conversion
- No personal data is sent to these services
- Only currency codes are used for rate queries

## Your Rights

- **Access**: You can view all your data within the app
- **Delete**: You can delete individual transactions, wallets, or all data by uninstalling the app
- **Export**: You can export your data to Excel format
- **Opt-Out**: You can disable personalized ads in your device settings

## Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.
